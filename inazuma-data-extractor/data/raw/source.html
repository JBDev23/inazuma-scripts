GENERAL KNOWLEDGE
There are a few things you need to know before playing this game.It doesn't 
matter if you didn't know about it.

1.You need to know how to read Japanese because some of the words I used here 
  are from Japanese Roman.So,if you didn't know how to read Japanese,the game 
  might be a little hard for you.Don't worry about Kanji though.I will 
  translated it for you.
2.For those who's using the real Nintendo DS,it's okay.For those who's using 
  an emulator like No$Gba or Desmume,it is reminded that you cannot use events
  that needs the Wi-Fi connection such as trading from IE2 to IE3.
3.I will sometimes used short forms like IE which is Inazuma Eleven.To avoid
  misunderstood,I will prepare a list of short forms used in this guide.

HAJIME ANO GEEMU
Start your game by pressing the first button you found.Put your name like 
Endou or something like that.Just put anything if you don't understand 
Japanese.

CONTROLS
Overworld
A      Button - The most important button.Confirms action.
B      Button - The decline button.Also the sprint button.
Y      Button - Does nothing
X      Button - Opens Menu
Start  Button - Quit the game without saving
Select Button - Does nothing
L/R    Button - Change camera angle
Stylus Mode   -> Tap an object to confirm action
              -> Sketch a line to make the character move,hold
              -> Click the bag to open menu

Battle/Match
A      Button - Moves right.
B      Button - Moves down.
Y      Button - Moves left.
X      Button - Moves up.
Start  Button - Still the quit button.
Select Button - Change the display of players.
L/R    Button - Hold to activate Burning Phase
Stylus Mode   -> Tap somewhere to make a PASS
              -> Tap own player to CHARGE
              -> Sketch a line from your own player to make it 
                 MOVE to the line you made

Nakama (Friends or teammate)
- This option allows you to look into your team's stat,skill and equipment.You
  can also change the number for each member.You can also make a member to 
  quit.

Mochimono (Inventory or items)
- It is divided to three things.Item,soubi(equipment) and hissatsu waza(skill)
  .Item is where you give the member some recovery item or other key items.
  Soubi is where you equip your member with shoes,bracelet and some kind of 
  pendant.You can also equip gloves for your goalkeeper.Hissatsu waza is where
  you give skill(if you have the skill sheet) to a member.Remind that giving 
  skill to a member makes consumes the sheet(maybe they eat it).You can also 
  browse your skill down here.

Senjyutsu (Formation)
- The senjyutsu option is divided according to the game.If its Bomber or Spark
  ,only three option available which is Story,Battle and Cuztomized.If its 
  Ogre,then Story,Battle and Cuztomized 1,2 and 3.Right,five options.You can
  choose whatsoever formation which is available to you on the F-thingy blue
  thing. You can also substitute players(in case you have never played known
  soccer before) from the bench.Sometime, the story mode will ask you to put
  someone to the field or not.I will let you guys know if it happens.

Jyouhou (Extra Things,not the exact translation)
- First option is your e-mail or blog to be exact. If you can read it,be my 
  guest.
- Second option is tutorial. Just learn from those pictures.
- Third option is scounting. If you have a scounting client waiting for you 
  and you forgot where and who,you can use this option but its 100% Japanese.
- Fourth option is Uniform. Your uniform can be changed but first you will 
  need to collect some uniforms. It shows what kind of shirt it is through 
  a preview.
- Fifth option is your team's status.Don't know much about it.
- Sixth option is the tournament's board.

Shitsutemu (Option mode,something like that)
- First option is your gameplay status like how long you have played,win ratio
  etc.
- Second option is HELP. Yes,help button. If you forget about something like
  how to activate Burning Phase,you can check here, but still, pictures.
- Last option is setting. First option in the setting is how B button will
  react.If you want to make the B Button a sprint button,then left choice or
  otherwise.Second option is sound,speaker or headphone.Last option is the
  volume of sound,BGM for background music and the other one is sound effect
  like "Kaboom" something like that.

Seebu (Save)
- Should I explain???Save your game,what else it does??

Nekketsu and Yuujou (Money and Coupon,something like that)
- No,both of these are not option. They just tell you how many money you have
  left.Nekketsu is money while Yuujou is more like a coupon voucher. The only
  way to gain either of these is through Battle,Match or selling.Oh and also
  opening chest.

ELEMENTS
Just like in every other game,this game also has elements.There are four
elements which are Fire,Wind,Wood and Earth.The element works in certain
condition.
- In an action situation(the situation where you bump with another opponent
  player),the stronger element always had the upper hand and if in the
  situation has two player from your own team which has the same element,the
  chances the action choosed is doubled.Most people(like me) didn't care much
  about the element because they are not so important if you only wanted to
  play the story mode.However in side quest,the elements sometimes is needed.
  Below are the diagram for which element is stronger against the other
  element.

EVOLUTION CHART
Depends on how many time you use a skill,it can be evolved if used lots,lots
of time.There will be a red message saying your skill leveled up if you used
your skill a lot.Also,Shoot Skill need to goal then counted as 1 point.
Example,Fire Tornado is used thus goaled,counted as 1 point.If the Fire 
Tornado doesn't goal,it doesn't count as 1 point for the evolution point.

/ BATTLE TUTORIAL /
///////////////////
From now on till the end of the game,actually there's not gonna be an end to
this game,you will encounter some battle while you are walking around.This
is called BATTLE MATCH where four people versus four opposing people.The match
has some specific rule sometime so depends on the colour of the name of the
battle like blue,green and red,I will tell you what kind of match you will
have and what you need to do to win.The title appears when you encounter a
battle match,after the flashing.Also,after you win,you can get some item
from them like skill sheet,food and drinks,or even uniform and equipment 
items.

BLUE TITLE
Whoever score 1,2 or 3 goal first wins(depends on the info on the upper 
screen).

GREEN TITLE
Steal the ball of the opposing team before time runs out.
